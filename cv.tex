\webpage{index}{Research}
% =====================================================================

I study how giant viruses repair their own DNA. Working with Prof.\ Kiran
Kondabagil, I have characterised the \textit{Mre11} homologs encoded by
\textit{Acanthamoeba polyphaga} mimivirus and Marseillevirus through their
biochemical and biophysical properties and their role in the viral life cycle.

My thesis work establishes that the mimivirus and Marseillevirus
\textit{Mre11} homologs are functional nucleases, defines their substrate
preferences and behaviour \textit{in vitro}, and connects that biochemistry
to viral genomic maintenance.

Apart from my thesis, I have collaborated on multiple projects resulting in publications and experiences in phage biology, microbiology, biosensors and human DNA repair systems.
